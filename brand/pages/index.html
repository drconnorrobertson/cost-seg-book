Explore the assumptions, trade-offs, and implementation details behind a planning decision.

01 / EXPLORE

Decide whether a cost segregation study belongs in your plan

The first-year deduction is one part of the decision. A complete comparison includes usable tax benefit, study and filing costs, state treatment, the holding period, and what happens when the property is sold.

Read more →02 / EXPLORE

Read a cost segregation report from basis to implementation

A polished report should allow the reviewer to trace the property facts, cost pool, methodology, classification, and final schedules. Begin with reconciliation before relying on the deduction summary.

Read more →03 / EXPLORE

Evaluate cost segregation across the expected holding period

A study can improve near-term cash flow while creating different tax timing later. A holding-period model makes the future assumptions explicit and shows whether the result remains useful if plans change.
